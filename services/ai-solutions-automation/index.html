Our approach

Start with the outcome your business needs

AI is useful when it solves a defined problem. We start by understanding how work is currently done, where delays or repetitive tasks occur and what information needs to move between people and systems. Then we assess whether automation or AI is appropriate.

01 · Plan

Workflow discovery and feasibility

We map the current process, identify repetitive steps and hand-offs, and define the expected outcome. We also consider data sensitivity, reliability, integration requirements and the need for human review.

02 · Create

AI-assisted business workflows

Potential applications include internal knowledge search, enquiry classification, document extraction, content assistance and customer-support workflows. The right solution depends on data quality, risk and the task being automated.

03 · Deliver

Integrations and notifications

Where supported, we can connect forms, CRM systems, spreadsheets and business applications so information reaches the right workflow. Access controls and failure handling should be designed from the outset.

04 · Improve

Testing, monitoring and handover

We test representative cases, document limitations and define how staff can review exceptions. AI outputs should be checked where accuracy, customer impact or sensitive information matters.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can AI automate every business task?

No. Some tasks require judgement, verified information or human approval. We recommend automation only where it is reliable and appropriate.

Can you connect AI with our existing software?

Possibly. Integration depends on the software's APIs, permissions, data format and technical constraints. We assess feasibility before committing to scope.

Will our data be safe?

Data handling, access, retention and third-party services must be reviewed for each project. We agree these requirements before implementation and do not recommend sending sensitive data to unapproved tools.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Maintenance and monitoring can be included as a separate agreed service.
