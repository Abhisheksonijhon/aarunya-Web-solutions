Our approach

Start with the outcome your business needs

Social media should do more than fill a calendar. It should explain what your business offers, build trust with the right audience and make it easy for interested people to take the next step. Aarunya plans social media around your brand, audience, resources and objectives.

01 · Plan

Content strategy and monthly planning

We define the audience, platforms, content pillars and communication priorities before preparing a practical monthly calendar. The plan balances educational, promotional, trust-building and engagement content.

02 · Create

Creative, captions and publishing

Depending on the agreed scope, we prepare post concepts, captions, story ideas and branded creative formats, then coordinate reviews and publishing. Every asset follows the approved brand direction and platform requirements.

03 · Deliver

Campaigns and audience engagement

We can coordinate promotional campaigns, seasonal communication and paid social campaign creative. Ad spend is separate from management fees. Community replies and lead handling are defined clearly so enquiries reach the right person.

04 · Improve

Reporting and continuous improvement

We review available reach, engagement, profile actions, website visits and enquiries. Insights guide the next content cycle; follower counts or sales are not guaranteed.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms can you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other suitable channels can be considered based on your audience and agreed scope.

Do you create post designs and captions?

Yes. Design and caption preparation can be included in the agreed monthly scope, with brand assets and approval responsibilities defined at onboarding.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Platform ad spend is paid separately by the client. Campaign setup and management are included only where specified in the proposal.

Can you guarantee followers or leads?

No. Outcomes depend on the offer, audience, creative, budget, competition and response process. We focus on consistent execution and measurable learning.
