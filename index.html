FAQs

Questions before getting started?

A few quick answers. If your project is unique, we're happy to discuss the details.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses and organisations looking for websites, online stores, marketing support, user experience design, or business software. The right scope depends on your goals and requirements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current structure and content, identify usability and performance priorities, and plan a redesign that retains the important parts of your existing site.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is part of the approach. Pages should adapt to phone, tablet, and desktop screens, with readable content and usable navigation.

Do you provide SEO and digital marketing?

Yes. SEO and digital marketing services can complement a website build or be considered as a separate project based on your current needs.

How do I get a quote?

Share a short description of your business, the service you're interested in, and any deadline or budget guidance. We'll use that to understand the scope and discuss the next step.
